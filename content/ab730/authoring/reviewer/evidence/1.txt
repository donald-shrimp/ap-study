Study guide for Exam AB-730: AI Business Professional
Feedback
Summarize this article for me
Purpose of this document
This study guide should help you understand what to expect on the exam
and includes a summary of the topics the exam might cover and links to
additional resources. The information and materials in this document
should help you focus your studies as you prepare for the exam.

Some exams are localized into other languages, and those are updated

Skills measured as of October 20, 2026
Audience profile
As a candidate for this Microsoft Certification, you should have
experience using generative AI-powered productivity tools, including
Microsoft 365 Copilot, Copilot Cowork, and pre-built agents. You take
advantage of AI and Work IQ to improve daily work, drive business
outcomes, and make informed decisions in business contexts without
building AI apps or writing code.
You should have experience using elements of generative AI, such as
prompt engineering, context engineering, and large language models, and
using Microsoft 365 Copilot tools such as notebooks and pages. You
should understand how these elements are used to drive more efficient
business outcomes.
You should be comfortable using Microsoft 365 core apps such as Outlook,
Word, Microsoft Teams, PowerPoint, and Excel. You should be familiar
with common business processes, including drafting emails, creating
presentations, generating images, and managing documents.
